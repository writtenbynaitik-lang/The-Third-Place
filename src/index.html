The idea

A room you don’t need a reason to be in.

The phrase is older than the restaurant. Sociologists use it for the public rooms a neighbourhood keeps between home and work — the ones you turn up to without an occasion, and leave having talked to someone.

Koregaon Park got one in 2025. It holds a wood-fired oven, a sommelier-led wine list, SOIL coffee, two small retail corners and a shelf of books, on one floor above Lane 5.

Simple things, done well.

The wine bar

Local legends, and the other kind.

Reported at its opening in 2025 as Pune’s first dedicated wine bar. The list is sommelier-led and pours by the glass, the carafe and the bottle.

Indian bottles are not a token section here — they have their own page, with their own tasting notes, and they sit on the same list as Chablis, Bordeaux and a Super Tuscan.

The room

Built partly from things that were already here.

Designed with stylist Kajal Patel around daylight, greenery, refurbished furniture and recycled materials — private corners at one end, communal counters at the other.

Daylight

Steel-framed glazing down one side, indoor trees, and terrazzo that takes the afternoon well.

Things to take home

Two retail corners share the floor — Altrove by Shreya Mantri and Style Mati by Fatima K. Punjabi.

Books

There are books on the shelves, and nobody minds how long you stay with one.
